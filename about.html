01 _ About

A mid-sized Maya city, read from the ground up.

Yaxché sits on the karst uplands of the central Petén. Between roughly 300 BCE and 900 CE it grew from a cluster of thatched compounds into a center of some eleven thousand people — then emptied, almost entirely, within two generations.
